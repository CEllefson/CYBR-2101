# Impact
The benefit of any business owner, manager, or data analyst to automate processes cannot be overstated, especially when the data input and output can be in the thousands or even millions which can eventually overwhelm an excel spreadsheet. The hope with this project is to create a template and guide that can be used by any manager to refine their approach when it comes to extracting data from the sales figures they have and use the new information to adjust their overarching strategies. The script I created takes in the sales, revenue and costs(these could be added to the script if needed but I felt were fine within this example) over a period of a month and automatically extrapolates useful metrics such as the Total Revenue, Total Cost, Total Profit, Average Profit, Max Profit, and Minimum Profit. 

As stated before this is an automated process that will take the sales figures from data.csv and will be exported to metrics.csv every 30 days, which allows for monthly overviews which can be further reviewed. For smaller businesses this can add valuable time that an owner can spend improving their craft and business, while for medium and large businesses this can become essential, where otherwise extrapolating data from hundreds of thousands of sales can quickly become cumbersome and take multiple people days to comb through. The man-hour reduction and data correctness through less human-error seems as an obvious benefit to any industry.
