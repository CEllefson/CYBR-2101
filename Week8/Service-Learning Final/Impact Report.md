# Impact
The benefit of any business owner, manager, or data analyst to automate processes cannot be overstated, especially when the data input and output can be in the thousands or even millions which can eventually overwhelm an excel spreadsheet. The hope with this project is to create a template and guide that can be used by any manager to refine their approach when it comes to extracting data from the sales figures they have and use the new information to adjust their overarching strategies. The script I created takes in the sales, revenue and costs(these could be added to the script if needed but I felt were fine within this example) over a period of a month and automatically extrapolates useful metrics such as the Total Revenue, Total Cost, Total Profit, Average Profit, Max Profit, and Minimum Profit. 

As stated before this is an automated process that will take the sales figures from data.csv and will be exported to metrics.csv every 30 days, which allows for monthly overviews which can be further reviewed. For smaller businesses this can add valuable time that an owner can spend improving their craft and business, while for medium and large businesses this can become essential, where otherwise extrapolating data from hundreds of thousands of sales can quickly become cumbersome and take multiple people days to comb through. The man-hour reduction and data correctness through less human-error seems as an obvious benefit to any industry.

Learning how scheduling operates and how it can be used is interesting. I often find that when we learn what we can do within a creative field, it often takes away creative boundaries and allows us to think through problems from new angles. I found learning more about CSVs and other data types to be interesting and allowed me to understand the flow of data from start to finish. Making this project even made me wonder about other parts of the project, such as how can they automatically update the data.csv, what other data can be extracted from the digested data and what trends can we find, and how can this be expanded upon. For this project I tried to keep it within scope and keep the program lean, especially if it will be running constantly for potentially years, allowing the manager or owner to open the file and see how things are going for their business from a topical perspective. 

# AI Concept Connections

Projects like this one where automation of easy, sometimes monotonous tasks can even benefit from an AI assistant to review data within these tables. The price cost analysis for the operation and delegation of these AI resources would highly depend on the size of the enterprise and the amount of data that needs to be parsed and digested, but for larger corporations that deal with data in the petabytes, having an AI agent that can use these automation scripts or larger programs can save thousands of man hours and easily justify the cost. This of course would require a human in the loop to ensure the AI is not hallucinating, which could cause more damage than benefit at these scales, but with the direction and push for AI integration at almost all levels this seems more of an inevitability than an option, so embracing and creating frameworks for safe, ethical, and secure AI use seems to be an appropriate approach. 

These projects can, for students, be an interesting method of learning how scripts can be created to automate processes and what can be automated within people's everyday lives, along with what are some things that companies could want to have automated. Learning these approaches and seeing how AI can be introduced is important to staying on the cutting edge of technology and not being left behind, as AI seems to be here to stay and not learning how to effectively use it only will hamstring individuals or entire enterprises.
